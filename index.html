    Timeline
    What it detects. Exact repeats, near-repeats (same tool, rephrased input), ping-pong between two tools, retries after errors, tool calls that never returned, and runs cut off by a step limit. Failures are sorted into five buckets: recovery policy, tool execution, tool selection, tool arguments, state orchestration.
    How well. On the TRAIL benchmark's human-labeled agent traces, Crescent Trace places about half of tool-failure and resource-abuse errors at the right agent step, with no LLM. The rules do not judge reasoning mistakes; read the timeline before you trust the verdict.
    Ask the dragon. An optional second opinion from Claude, on your own Claude account. Only the flagged steps and their neighbours are sent, and only when you click. The free rule-based diagnosis never leaves your browser.
